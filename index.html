Abstract

Existing audio forgery detection and localization methods often overfit dataset-specific low-level artifacts, limiting their generalization to subtle, localized, and unseen manipulations. Recent audio large language model approaches cast the task as question answering but still model forensic evidence implicitly, without linking manipulation cues to predictions.

ThinkOmni is a reasoning-driven omni-modal large language model that jointly performs explicit forensic reasoning, spoofing detection, and temporal manipulation localization. We construct Forensic-Aware Chain-of-Thought (FACoT), a 100K-sample dataset with structured forensic evidence and reasoning annotations. Forensic-Aware Modality-Incremental Learning progressively aligns semantic, acoustic, and spectral-visual representations with the LLM backbone, while Forensic-Consistent Multi-task Loss coordinates reasoning generation, detection, and localization.

ThinkOmni Framework

ThinkOmni is built on Qwen2.5-Omni and progressively integrates complementary forensic evidence. Semantic Forensic Adaptation first establishes semantic reasoning, Acoustic Forensic Augmentation introduces fine-grained acoustic evidence through SAFE, and Multi-modal Forensic Refinement adds spectrogram-based visual cues while preserving previously aligned encoders.

FACoT Dataset

FACoT contains 100K samples aggregated from eight public benchmarks across fully real, fully fake, and partially fake audio. Its human-machine collaborative pipeline combines seed chain-of-thought annotation, expert-defined verification, large-scale SFT-based expansion, and CLAP-based filtering for audio-text consistency.

Semantic-Acoustic Forensic Enhancer

SAFE aligns semantic and acoustic evidence through a local cross-attention branch for fine-grained cue interaction and a global forgery discriminator for utterance-level forensic context. A learned gate fuses both branches before the evidence enters the Thinker backbone.

Experimental Results

93.70%Intra-dataset ACC
85.15%Cross-dataset F1
88.05%Intra-dataset mAP
74.67%Cross-dataset mAP

ThinkOmni achieves the strongest reported average localization performance under both intra- and cross-dataset evaluation. The ablation results show that FCML and sample-specific reasoning improve temporal evidence modeling, while shuffled rationales degrade detection and localization.

Cross-dataset claims are limited to ADD 2023 Track 2 and Speech-Forensics, the two held-out benchmarks evaluated in the paper.

Case Studies

Representative examples show how ThinkOmni organizes acoustic, prosodic, environmental, and semantic cues into an inspectable rationale while producing an authenticity class and temporal localization result.
