Why ThinkOmni

From a verdict to inspectable forensic reasoning

Existing systems often learn dataset-specific artifacts and return a label or boundary without explaining the evidence behind it. ThinkOmni connects semantic inconsistencies, acoustic artifacts, and spectral-visual cues to three coordinated outputs: a forensic rationale, an authenticity class, and manipulated time intervals.

ReasonExplain the evidence
DetectReal, fully fake, or partially fake
LocalizeFind one or more forged intervals

FACoT dataset

100K samples with structured forensic supervision

FACoT aggregates eight public source datasets across fully real, fully fake, and partially fake audio. A human-machine collaborative pipeline creates structured rationales, followed by expert-defined quality control and CLAP-based grounding filters.

100Kaudio samples
8training sources
3authenticity classes
3 levelssemantic, acoustic, temporal

Method

Progressive omni-modal forensic alignment

Built on Qwen2.5-Omni, ThinkOmni adds a dedicated acoustic pathway and aligns evidence progressively instead of forcing all modalities to adapt at once.

01

FMIL

Progressively aligns semantic, acoustic, and spectral-visual representations while reducing interference among heterogeneous modalities.

02

SAFE

Combines local cross-attention with a global forgery discriminator to preserve fine-grained artifacts and long-range forensic context.

03

FCML

Balances reasoning, detection, and localization with role-aware token weights and condition-adaptive temporal boundary loss.

Semantic-Acoustic Forensic Enhancer

Local evidence, global context

SAFE lets acoustic features query semantic evidence at fine temporal resolution, while a global branch models utterance-level inconsistency. A learned gate fuses both views before they enter the reasoning backbone.

Qualitative analysis

Reasoning that can be inspected

ThinkOmni organizes acoustic, prosodic, environmental, and semantic cues into a rationale while producing the class and temporal boundaries. These cases illustrate behavior rather than establish reliability on their own.
